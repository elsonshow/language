Synonyms / Antonyms

Learn common synonyms and antonyms to enrich your vocabulary.

Common Synonyms

  • Big - Large, Huge, Enormous
  • Small - Tiny, Little, Miniature
  • Happy - Joyful, Delighted, Pleased
  • Sad - Unhappy, Depressed, Miserable
  • Fast - Quick, Rapid, Speedy
  • Slow - Gradual, Leisurely, Sluggish
  • Beautiful - Attractive, Gorgeous, Stunning
  • Ugly - Unattractive, Hideous, Repulsive
  • Smart - Intelligent, Clever, Bright
  • Dumb - Stupid, Foolish, Idiotic

Common Antonyms

  • Hot - Cold
  • Day - Night
  • Good - Bad
  • Right - Wrong
  • Big - Small
  • Up - Down
  • Happy - Sad
  • Fast - Slow
  • Old - Young
  • Rich - Poor

Academic Words

Important words for academic writing and study.

Common Academic Words

  • Analyze - To examine in detail
  • Evaluate - To assess the value or quality
  • Argument - A reason or set of reasons
  • Hypothesis - A proposed explanation
  • Methodology - A system of methods
  • Conclusion - A judgment reached
  • Evidence - Facts or information supporting a claim
  • Theory - A well-substantiated explanation
  • Concept - An abstract idea
  • Context - The circumstances surrounding a text or event

Business Words

Essential vocabulary for business and professional contexts.

Common Business Words

  • Budget - A financial plan for a period
  • Profit - Financial gain from a business activity
  • Loss - Financial deficit from a business activity
  • Investment - Putting money into something for profit
  • Market - A place where goods are bought and sold
  • Strategy - A plan of action to achieve a goal
  • Client - A person or organization using a service
  • Customer - A person who buys goods or services
  • Competition - Rivalry between businesses
  • Innovation - The introduction of new ideas or methods

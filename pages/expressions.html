Useful Expressions

Discover practical English expressions for various situations. From daily conversations to business meetings, these phrases will help you communicate confidently.

Expression Categories

  • Daily Conversation
  • Work & Business
  • Travel
  • Shopping
  • Social Expressions

Daily Conversation

Essential phrases for everyday interactions.

Greetings & Introductions

  • Good morning/afternoon/evening!
  • How are you doing today?
  • Nice to meet you!
  • My name is [Name].
  • Where are you from?

Small Talk

  • What do you do for a living?
  • How's the weather today?
  • Did you have a good weekend?
  • What are your hobbies?
  • Have you seen any good movies lately?

Work & Business

Professional expressions for workplace communication.

Meetings

  • Let's get started with the meeting.
  • Could you please clarify that point?
  • I agree with what you're saying.
  • Let's schedule a follow-up meeting.
  • What's your opinion on this matter?

Email Communication

  • Thank you for your email.
  • I'm writing to inquire about...
  • Please find attached the document you requested.
  • I'll get back to you as soon as possible.
  • Best regards,

Travel

Useful phrases for traveling abroad.

At the Airport

  • Where is the check-in counter?
  • Can I have a window seat?
  • How do I get to the gate?
  • Is there a duty-free shop here?
  • My flight has been delayed.

Accommodation

  • I have a reservation under the name [Name].
  • Could you please bring extra towels?
  • What time is breakfast served?
  • How do I connect to the Wi-Fi?
  • Could I check out late?

Shopping

Phrases for shopping and making purchases.

Asking for Help

  • Where can I find [item]?
  • Do you have this in a different size?
  • What colors does this come in?
  • Can I try this on?
  • Do you have any discounts?

Making a Purchase

  • I'll take this, please.
  • Can I pay by credit card?
  • Could I have a receipt, please?
  • Does this come with a warranty?
  • Can I return this if it doesn't fit?

Social Expressions

Phrases for social gatherings and events.

Inviting & Accepting

  • Would you like to come over for dinner?
  • I'd love to, thank you!
  • I'm sorry, I can't make it.
  • What time should I arrive?
  • Should I bring anything?

Giving Compliments

  • You look great today!
  • That's a wonderful idea!
  • You did an excellent job!
  • This food is delicious!
  • I really like your [item].

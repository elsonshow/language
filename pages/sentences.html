Sentence Examples

Explore a wide range of English sentence examples for different contexts and sentence structures. These examples will help you understand how to construct effective sentences.

Sentence Categories

  • Simple Sentences
  • Complex Sentences
  • Business Sentences
  • Email Sentences
  • Daily Phrases

Simple Sentences

Simple sentences contain one independent clause with a subject and a verb.

Examples

  • The sun shines brightly.
  • She reads a book every night.
  • They play football on weekends.
  • I drink coffee in the morning.
  • The cat sleeps on the couch.
  • He works in a big company.
  • We visit our grandparents monthly.
  • She sings beautifully.
  • The flowers bloom in spring.
  • They walk to school together.

Complex Sentences

Complex sentences contain one independent clause and at least one dependent clause.

Examples

  • Although it was raining, we went for a walk.
  • She studies hard because she wants to pass the exam.
  • When the bell rings, the students go to class.
  • I'll call you as soon as I arrive home.
  • Since he was sick, he stayed in bed all day.
  • We can't go out unless we finish our homework.
  • She was happy that she got the job.
  • Before you leave, please turn off the lights.
  • The party will be fun if everyone comes.
  • I'll help you with your project when I have time.

Business Sentences

Professional sentences for business communication.

Examples

  • The quarterly report shows significant growth.
  • We need to increase our market share by 10%.
  • Please review the proposal and provide feedback.
  • Our team will meet the deadline as promised.
  • We're expanding our operations to new markets.
  • The new product launch was a great success.
  • We're implementing new strategies to improve efficiency.
  • Please contact the client to schedule a meeting.
  • We need to reduce costs without compromising quality.
  • The presentation will be delivered tomorrow morning.

Email Sentences

Common sentences used in email communication.

Examples

  • Thank you for your prompt response.
  • I'm writing to follow up on our previous conversation.
  • Please find attached the document you requested.
  • I apologize for the delay in getting back to you.
  • Could you please provide more information about this matter?
  • I look forward to hearing from you soon.
  • Please let me know if you need any further assistance.
  • I'm cc'ing the relevant team members on this email.
  • We're pleased to inform you that your request has been approved.
  • Best regards,

Daily Phrases

Common phrases used in everyday conversations.

Examples

  • Good morning! How did you sleep?
  • What's for breakfast today?
  • I'm running late for work.
  • Could you pass the salt, please?
  • What's on TV tonight?
  • I need to do some grocery shopping.
  • How was your day?
  • Would you like to go out for dinner?
  • I'm tired. I think I'll go to bed early.
  • See you tomorrow!

150-word Stories

Short, engaging stories to practice your reading skills.

The Kind Neighbor

One cold winter morning, Sarah noticed her elderly neighbor, Mr. Thompson, struggling to carry his groceries. She immediately went outside to help. "Thank you, Sarah," he said with a smile. "You're always so kind." Sarah helped him carry the groceries inside and even made him a cup of hot tea. Mr. Thompson told her stories about his youth, and Sarah listened attentively. Before leaving, Sarah promised to check on him every week. Mr. Thompson felt grateful for her kindness, and Sarah felt happy to have made a difference. Small acts of kindness, they both realized, can brighten someone's day and create lasting friendships.

A Day at the Park

Last weekend, Lisa and her family went to the park. The sun was shining, and the birds were singing. Lisa played on the swings with her little brother, while her parents had a picnic. They ate sandwiches, fruits, and cookies. After lunch, they went for a walk around the lake. They saw ducks swimming and children flying kites. Lisa even made a new friend, a girl named Emma who was visiting from another city. They played tag and shared their favorite toys. As the day ended, the sky turned pink and orange. Lisa's family packed up their things and headed home. Lisa felt tired but happy. It was a perfect day filled with laughter, fun, and new memories.

News-style Reading

Simple news articles to practice reading about current events.

Local Community Center Opens New Library

The Greenfield Community Center opened a new library yesterday. The library has over 5,000 books, including children's stories, novels, and non-fiction books. It also has computers with internet access for public use. The mayor attended the opening ceremony and gave a speech. "This library is a wonderful addition to our community," he said. "It will provide a place for people of all ages to learn and grow." Many residents came to the opening. Children were excited about the colorful children's section, while adults were happy to have a quiet place to read. The library will be open from 9 AM to 7 PM every day. It also plans to host storytime for children and book clubs for adults. The community is looking forward to using this new resource.

New Recycling Program Launches in Town

The town of Rivertown has launched a new recycling program. Starting next month, residents will be able to recycle more items, including plastic containers, glass bottles, and cardboard boxes. The town has distributed new recycling bins to every household. A local environmental group organized a workshop to teach residents about the new program. "Recycling is important for our planet," said the group's leader. "By recycling more, we can reduce waste and protect the environment." The town hopes that this new program will increase recycling rates by 30% in the next year. Residents are encouraged to sort their waste carefully and follow the new recycling guidelines. The town government believes this program will make Rivertown a greener and more sustainable community.

Simple Articles with Questions

Read these articles and answer the questions to test your comprehension.

The Benefits of Exercise

Exercise is good for both your body and mind. When you exercise, your body releases endorphins, which are chemicals that make you feel happy. Regular exercise can help you maintain a healthy weight and reduce the risk of many diseases, such as heart disease and diabetes. It also strengthens your muscles and bones. Exercise can improve your sleep quality and increase your energy levels. Additionally, exercise is a great way to reduce stress and anxiety. You don't need to do intense workouts to get these benefits. Even simple activities like walking, cycling, or gardening can help. Try to exercise for at least 30 minutes most days of the week. Your body and mind will thank you!

Questions:

  1. What chemicals does your body release when you exercise?
  2. How can exercise help with your weight?
  3. Name two diseases that exercise can help prevent.
  4. How much exercise should you try to do each day?

How to Save Water

Water is a precious resource, and it's important to save it. Here are some simple ways to save water at home. First, turn off the tap while brushing your teeth or shaving. This can save up to 8 gallons of water per day. Second, take shorter showers. Try to keep your showers under 5 minutes. Third, fix any leaky faucets. A dripping faucet can waste up to 20 gallons of water per day. Fourth, use a broom instead of a hose to clean your driveway or sidewalk. Fifth, only run the dishwasher or washing machine when you have a full load. Sixth, water your plants in the early morning or evening to reduce evaporation. By following these tips, you can save water and help protect the environment.

Questions:

  1. Why is it important to save water?
  2. How much water can you save by turning off the tap while brushing your teeth?
  3. How long should you try to keep your showers?
  4. When is the best time to water plants?
